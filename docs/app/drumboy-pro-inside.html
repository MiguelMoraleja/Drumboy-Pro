A patch, not a chain

Firmware V1.05+ replaces the fixed chain with a small patch. Every source (10 layers, the metronome, line-in) picks where it enters, and every module picks where its output goes: a later module or straight to MASTER. Modules always run in the same order (EQ, Filter 1, Filter 2, Effect 1, Effect 2, Reverb), so a patch can never loop. The default patch is the old serial chain.

On the device: a layer's OUT is on its third instrument page (hold I and press the layer pad, then tap I twice), module outputs and the metronome are on the ROUTING page (press MIXER twice), line-in is the mixer's MIXER IN.

Volume

What one hit's volume is made of

Every 0–100 setting is cubed

Cubed controls. Level, master, pan and fill velocity all go through the same x³ table, so the top half of each knob does most of the work: 50 is about −18 dB, not −6 dB.

Fill velocities. Fill hits use 100 (high), 75 (the quiet STEP hits) and 40 (the bottom of DESCEND, ASCEND and RANDOM). GHOST is a single hit at 50.

Pitch. See the panel below: from V1.11 pitch is set in semitones.

Soft clip and LIMITER. After master and pan the output is bent smoothly toward a ceiling instead of clipping hard. LIMITER on the mixer page sets that ceiling from −10 to +0 dB (V1.10+); the bend starts at 2/3 of it. At +0 dB, the default, that is 0.667 (−3.5 dBFS), as before V1.10.

Pitch in semitones (V1.11)

On the device: PITCH on instrument page 1 (hold I and press a pad). One detent = one semitone; a fast turn jumps to the next octave line (−24, −12, 0, +12, +24). Holding ADD while turning still picks the pitch LFO.

Speed, not time-stretch. Pitch plays the sample faster or slower: +12 is twice as fast and half as long, −12 half as fast and twice as long. Range 0.25× to 4×.

Older kits and songs keep their exact old pitch, shown the old way (e.g. 1.37), until you turn PITCH; then it snaps to the nearest semitone. Files saved by V1.11 also keep a close old-style value, so older firmware still plays about the right pitch.

LFO on pitch moves up to ±12 semitones at full depth (old-style layers keep the old rule). Above +12 bright samples can sound harsh: the player interpolates linearly, without anti-alias filtering.

Sample preview (V1.11)

On an instrument page, turning SAMPLE plays the selected sample a quarter of a second after the knob stops, straight from the SD card: no loading, no wait, and long samples play in full (loaded samples stop at 5.44 s). The layer pad replays it while the selected sample isn't the loaded one. Nothing changes in the layer until ADD loads it.

PREVIEW (on/off) and PREV LEVEL are on the Metronome page, bottom row. The preview goes straight to the output, before master volume, and stops when you leave the instrument pages, change layer or press ADD.

Euclid

Spreads HITS as evenly as possible over STEPS, then repeats the pattern from the song start to the song end. Same algorithm as the firmware (V1.10).

Mode

Grey beats are the layer's beats from before (one on beats 1 and 3, the first with a WAVE fill). MERGE keeps them; REPLACE drops them. ACCENTS spreads that many accents evenly over the hits; the other hits become GHOST notes (half velocity, about −18 dB). OFF keeps every hit full.

The SONG page on the device

On the device

⁘
Tap the SONG key on its own (bottom right, the one you hold with the pads to pick a bank): the SONG page. Tap it there to turn the chain on or off (CHAIN).
↻
Fields: STEP picks the step to edit, BANK and REPEATS set it, LENGTH adds or removes steps at the end (a new step copies the last one), END is LOOP or STOP, CHAIN ON/OFF.
⁘+
Picking a bank by hand (SONG + pads) never turns the chain off. A bank the chain uses: the chain jumps to its step (from the loop end while playing). Any other bank plays one loop as a detour, then the chain goes on from where it was; the detour doesn't count as a repeat. Picked while stopped, it plays first. (V1.14; before, a bank outside the chain turned it off.)
⧉
Copy a whole bank: on the SONG page hold COPY (all 10 layers of the active bank, beats and fills), pick another bank with SONG + pads, hold PASTE. Works while playing; the per-layer copy on the song page is unchanged.
■
STOP keeps the chain's position; RESET goes back to step 1. The chain is saved with the song; older firmware ignores it.

Per-note settings

Each beat can have its own velocity, chance, condition and nudge (the NOTE page). Pick a step, change it, and hear the loops go by. V1.14.

Selected note
One bar of 16ths: click a step to select it (or add a beat). Bar height = velocity; a ring = played this loop, faded = skipped.
The NOTE page on the device

On the device

S
Open it: on a layer's song page tap LAYER SONG (S) twice: song → EUCLID → NOTE → song. NOTE picks the beat (it is highlighted on the timeline).
♪
VELOCITY 1–127 for that beat; until you turn it, the beat plays as FLAT, GHOST or MUTED as before. On a fill it scales every hit. MIDI recording stores each note's velocity here.
%
CHANCE 0–100 % in 5 % steps, rolled every loop, on top of the layer's PROB. A fill plays or not as a whole.
?
CONDITION: A:B plays on loop A of every B (1:4 = first of four), !A:B the other loops, 1ST only the first loop after START or a bank change, PRE if the layer's last conditioned beat played, FKEY only while you hold the F (FILL) key, and !FKEY only when you don't; the screen shows FKEY while it is held. It is not the layer's FILL switch (F + pad).
▮
In the lanes you can see these settings on every layer: a beat's height is its velocity (FLAT full, GHOST two thirds), a hollow beat has a chance below 100 %, and a yellow dot above marks a condition. A layer that does not sound (muted, or another layer is soloed) is dimmed.
↔
NUDGE plays a plain beat up to 12 % of a 16th early or late, without moving it on the grid. Fills keep their timing.
↶
UNDO / REDO: MUTE (the M on the left) + LEFT undoes, MUTE + RIGHT redoes the last beat or bank edit, on any page and while playing: MOVE, SHIFT ALL, insert, CLEAR, fills, paste, whole-bank paste, EUCLID, these NOTE settings and a recording take. Up to 64 steps; one knob gesture or one EUCLID visit is one step. Knob settings of the sound are not undone. LOAD, NEW and changing MEASURE/BAR/QUANTIZE start a new history.
ADD
ADD + turn changes every beat of the bank by the same amount (CONDITION: sets them all). CLEAR resets the selected beat, ADD + CLEAR the whole bank. Songs from older firmware play exactly as before.

Following a MIDI clock

Set the master's tempo (Loopy Pro often has decimals, because it comes from the length of the first loop) and compare how far the Drumboy drifts. V1.13.

Velocity, in and out
How far behind or ahead of the master (milliseconds)

On the device

⏱
MIDI IN with clock (I/O page): the Drumboy plays only as far as each incoming clock allows and restarts its own timer on it. It can't run ahead, it is at most 1/24 of a beat behind, and nothing adds up over time. Before V1.13 it only copied the tempo (rounded to whole BPM) and then ran on its own.
⟲
No MIDI loops: while it follows a clock it sends no clock and no start/stop of its own on MIDI OUT. Before, a setup that echoed MIDI OUT back to MIDI IN doubled the tempo and ran away to 200 BPM. The sync jack follows the incoming clock.
♪
Notes play at their velocity; chords and fast runs (running status) all play; a note with velocity 0 is silent. Recording keeps soft notes (velocity below 64) as GHOST. MIDI OUT sends each hit's real velocity: FLAT 127, GHOST 64, fills as they play.
↯
CRASH k AT address (V1.14): if the firmware crashes it now restarts by itself instead of freezing, and shows this until ADD or CLEAR. The details are added to System/Crash.log on the SD card: send that line to find the cause.
!
ERR nnnn instead of SLAVE on the Rhythm page counts MIDI bytes lost on the way in. It should stay at SLAVE.

Autosave

⏲
AUTOSAVE on the Metronome page (last field): OFF, 2, 5, 10 (default) or 20 minutes. When that time has passed and the song changed, it is written to the SD card: when stopped, or at a loop end while playing. With MIDI notes coming in it waits for STOP, so a note is never held back. It also waits while you turn knobs or press keys.
⏻
After a power cut or a crash: at power-on the Drumboy asks RESTORE AUTOSAVE?. ADD loads it, CLEAR throws it away. Then save it into a slot as usual.
⇄
LOAD and NEW autosave unsaved work first, so a wrong LOAD can be undone at the next power-on. SAVE into a slot removes the autosave files (the work is safe in the slot).
◌
SAVE on screen (V1.14): while an autosave writes, the status area shows SAVE; keys, the screen and incoming MIDI notes wait for that moment. SDERR in red means the write failed. The Metronome page shows under AUTOSAVE how many milliseconds the last one took.
✓
Two files (System/File/Autosave_A.rwf and _B) are written in turn and sealed with a checksum, so pulling the power in the middle of a write still leaves the previous copy. Your numbered slots are never touched.

Releases and changes

Every firmware version of this fork (MiguelMoraleja/Drumboy-Pro), newest first. This tab and the others are updated with every release.

To update: copy one Drumboy_Pro_Fw_01_YY.bin to the SD card root (delete older ones), check its size matches exactly, and power on. A smaller file means a cut-off copy (the bootloader shows SIZE-CHECK).

Built from the Drumboy Pro firmware source (Controller.cpp, Global.h): fill tables, level rules, the routing patch (Routing.h), Euclidean patterns, accents and beat editing (Euclid.h, BeatList.h), pitch (Pitch.h), song mode (SongChain.h), MIDI clock and velocity (MidiClock.h, MidiParser.h), autosave (Autosave.h), per-note settings (BeatNote.h), undo (UndoLog.h) and volume math match the code. The test sounds are synthesized in the browser; the device plays your samples.
